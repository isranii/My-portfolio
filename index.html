A model that tells you whether India can chase the target in cricket (still screams at the TV, but now there's data). A system that catches fraud before the fraud catches you.

What I actually want to do is business consulting. Walk into a room, look at a mess of numbers, say something sharp, and walk out with a plan people can actually use. Won three ideathons doing exactly this. Each time I couldn't quite believe it worked. But it did.
